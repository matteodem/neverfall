# Character Creator Descriptions

Short flavor text for the character creator. Display the selected species and class descriptions beneath their respective options.

## Game Classes

### Warrior
A fearless melee fighter who excels in close combat. Warriors rely on strength and durability to survive even the toughest battles.

- **Combat:** Melee
- **Role:** Fighter
- **Difficulty:** Easy

### Ranger
A skilled marksman who attacks enemies from a distance. Rangers rely on precision and mobility to stay one step ahead of danger.

- **Combat:** Ranged
- **Role:** Damage
- **Difficulty:** Medium

### Mage
A master of arcane magic who unleashes powerful spells against enemies. Mages specialize in magical damage and controlling the battlefield.

- **Combat:** Ranged Magic
- **Role:** Damage
- **Difficulty:** Medium

## Species

### Human
Adaptable and ambitious, humans carve their own paths through Neverfall. Their determination helps them thrive in an ever-changing world.

### Ashborn
Forged by ash and hardship, the Ashborn are resilient survivors with a fierce spirit and a mysterious past.

### Sylvan
Deeply connected to the forests, the Sylvan are graceful guardians who live in harmony with nature and its ancient magic.
